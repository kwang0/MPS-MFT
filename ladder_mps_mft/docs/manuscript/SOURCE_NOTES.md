## Interpretive boundaries carried into the draft

- Mean-field iteration is not physical time. A response multiplier or slow
  numerical wall shift is not a measured excitation frequency.
- A large bare pairing response is not a phase boundary when the normal
  reference is not self-consistent and the projected basis leaks strongly.
- The even-particle charge curvature is not the pair-breaking denominator.
- Opposite leg/rung pair signs do not establish a pair-density wave.
- The ideal wall-filling inference from the finite stripe wavelength is
  conditional; no integrated wall charge or thermodynamic filling is claimed.
- The chi=400 energy difference retains the user's September 6 working
  interpretation alongside the original unresolved formal acceptance.
- Local pairing in the eventual stripe state is an open question; the
  disappearing anomalous expectation value does not answer it alone.
