5. The Stage 2 pilot is deliberately staged

Prepare
Hash-check Stage 1 and orthonormalize 14 named candidates to 12 directions.
Re-reference
Strict h=0 number-conserving solve, then a parity-only pairing reference.
Discover
Nine normal and three pairing probes at h=1e-4; reuse responses across three geometries.
Gate
Require convergence, 5% reciprocity, and 5% normal/pair decoupling before validation.

The five familiar q=0 pair labels contain only three independent fields: onsite, rung, and leg. Extended-s and d-wave are combinations within that span. Keeping their labels in provenance while eliminating exact linear dependence makes the response solve cheaper without restricting the mixed eigenvector.

A fresh strict h=0 reference is not cosmetic. Dividing a shared residual relaxation by h=1e-4 would contaminate every finite-difference column and can visibly break reciprocity. Pairing probes branch from the same re-relaxed state after removing only Nf; fermion parity and Sz remain conserved.

All three geometry kernels use the newly measured backbone hole binding, -0.146510221, for their prefactor; the older registry interpolation is retained only as model provenance.

Discovery reserves at most 18.656 CPU node-hours. The optional three-mode h/h2 validation reserves at most 9.609 more and is a separate operator decision. Each validation task performs its two amplitudes sequentially and checks 5% linearity before Richardson extrapolation.
